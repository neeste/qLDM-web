Operator Hardware & Analog Gain Check

Because qLDM uses a lower digital reference than qCLS to accommodate the 10-dB crest factor and insert-phone roll-off of 100 dB SPL five-tone complexes, verify that your hardware attenuator / output gain is set to the qLDM setting (and remember to restore the qCLS setting before running qCLS).

Listener Instructions

“You will hear two sounds. Choose the one that was louder. Judge the loudness of the whole sound, not of a part of it, and ignore differences in pitch or quality.”

Practice Complete — Tester Comprehension Check

Confirm they compared the overall loudness of the whole sound (and did not try to listen out a single component pitch).
